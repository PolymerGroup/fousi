About Peflag

About PEFLAG

PEFLAG is the trading brand of Shandong Fousi International Trade Co., Ltd. We buy, test and export a deliberately narrow range of metabolic research peptides — incretins, amylin analogues, mitochondrial peptides and lipolytic fragments — for laboratories, CROs and distributors worldwide.

Why a narrow catalogue

Most Chinese peptide suppliers list several hundred compounds. That breadth is impressive on a website and expensive in practice: stock rotates slowly, batches age, and the sales team cannot answer a technical question about any single molecule. We went the other way.

Seventeen metabolic compounds, each with its full strength ladder on one page, stocked in depth. When a researcher asks why the 40 mg retatrutide vial is priced against the 30 mg rather than the 50 mg, or whether AOD9604 and hGH fragment 176-191 are the same molecule, someone who knows answers — usually within the hour.

That focus also shapes what we refuse. We do not sell finished products, we do not offer dosing guidance, and we do not describe any compound as a treatment. Every invoice, label and certificate carries the same line: for laboratory research use only, not for human or veterinary use.

Where we sit in the chain

We are an exporter, not a synthesis plant. The peptides we ship come from GMP-aligned manufacturers in Shandong and Jiangsu that we work with on repeat batch schedules, and every batch is tested against the release criteria on our quality page before it enters stock. Our work is the part between the reactor and your bench: batch selection, analytical verification, documentation, packaging and the customs paperwork that determines whether a parcel actually arrives.

How we work

Four commitments that decide whether you reorder

Documentation first

The COA arrives before the invoice, not with the goods. If the batch does not meet your specification you decline it at no cost.

One specification

≥99% HPLC, ESI-MS identity, ≤5% water. The same criteria apply to a 2 mg vial and a 120 mg vial, and to your first order and your tenth.

Shipments that arrive

Express only, tracked, neutral outer carton, paperwork prepared for the destination. Held parcels get new documents the same day.

A named contact

Your quote, your batch and your tracking come from the same person on WhatsApp or email — not from a shared inbox with a ticket number.

Compliance posture

What we will and will not do

Research chemical export is regulated differently in every destination. Our position is deliberately conservative, because a supplier that cuts corners on compliance is a supply risk to your programme.

  • Label every unit research-use onlyAlways
  • State chemical identity and purityAlways
  • Provide dosing or clinical guidanceNever
  • Sell as medicine, supplement or cosmeticNever
  • Ship to private individualsNo — institutions only
For Laboratory Research Use Only. Not for Human or Veterinary Use. Products are supplied as reference materials for in-vitro and pre-clinical research. They are not medicines, supplements, cosmetics or diagnostic agents, and must be handled only by qualified laboratory personnel.

Buyer responsibility

By ordering, the buyer confirms that they are a qualified researcher or an institution purchasing for laboratory work, that the compound is legal to import for research in their jurisdiction, and that it will not be administered to humans or animals or resold as a consumer product.
