Method 01 — purity

Reversed-phase HPLC, and why the number is only half the story

Every batch is run on a C18 column with a water/acetonitrile gradient containing 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid, detected at 220 nm and integrated by area normalisation. The main peak must reach 99.0% or the batch is not released.

Chromatographic purity, however, is not the same as peptide content. A vial can be 99.4% pure and still contain 4% moisture and a trifluoroacetate counter-ion. That is why the COA also reports Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range per vial — the three numbers together are what let you calculate an accurate molar concentration when you reconstitute.

  • Column: C18, 4.6 × 250 mm, 5 µm
  • Detection: 220 nm (peptide bond)
  • Gradient: 5–65% acetonitrile over 30 min
  • Acceptance: ≥99.0% main peak, no single impurity >0.5%

Method 02 — identity

Mass spectrometry catches what HPLC cannot

A pure but wrong peptide will pass an HPLC check. Electrospray ionisation mass spectrometry is the test that proves the sequence — and the only reliable way to detect a blended or substituted batch.

How the check works

The sample is infused and the multiply charged envelope is deconvoluted to a single neutral mass. That mass is compared with the theoretical value for the stated sequence. A deletion, an extra glycine or a missing fatty-acid chain shifts the mass by tens of daltons — far outside the ±0.1% acceptance window.

For disulfide-containing peptides such as AOD9604 we run reduced and non-reduced LC-MS and compare, because a broken bridge does not change the nominal mass.

Packaging & logistics

From the fill line to your bench

Primary packing

Ten 10 mL borosilicate serum vials with butyl stoppers and aluminium crimp seals, each labelled with compound, strength, batch and the research-use notice.

Transit protection

Tamper-evident tray, shock-absorbing foam, desiccant and an insulated layer where climate requires it. No cold packs for lyophilized powder — moisture control matters more than temperature.

Discretion

The outer carton names no product and carries no institutional branding. Only the consignee appears on the label, as required by the carrier.

Carriers & timing

DHL, FedEx and UPS express. Dispatch within 24–48 h of cleared funds; 5–9 working days in transit to North America and the EU; 3–6 days to Asia-Pacific.

Customs package

Commercial invoice, packing list, COA and MSDS, declared as research chemicals under the correct tariff heading with an accurate unit value.

If something goes wrong

Detained parcels are resolved with replacement paperwork the same day; lost or destroyed consignments are reshipped or credited. That commitment is written on the invoice.

Buyer questions

Quality and documentation FAQ

Can I see the COA before paying?

Yes. The COA for the exact batch is sent for review before any invoice is paid. It states the batch number, HPLC purity with the chromatogram, ESI-MS deconvoluted mass, water content and fill-weight range. If the data does not meet your specification, you decline the batch at no cost.

Are your peptides tested by an independent laboratory?

Every batch is tested in-house, and independent third-party testing is available on request. For programmes that require external verification, we submit a sample to an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name; the fee and five-day turnaround are quoted up front.

What purity specification do you guarantee?

≥99.0% by reversed-phase HPLC at 220 nm, area normalised. Batches below that threshold are held back rather than sold at a discount through another channel. Purity is chromatographic purity of the peptide peak and does not include counter-ions, moisture or bulking agents, which is why water content and fill weight are reported separately.

How is the cold chain handled for lyophilized powder?

Lyophilized powder does not require frozen transit; it requires dry, moderate conditions. Kits ship with desiccant and an insulated layer where the destination climate demands it, and the outer carton is neutral. On arrival, store at -20 °C and let the vial equilibrate to room temperature before opening to prevent condensation on the powder.

Do you provide endoxin and sterility data?

Endotoxin (LAL) and sterility testing can be added to any batch on request. They are not part of the standard release because most research customers do not need them, but for cell-culture work the data is often required — ask for it at quotation stage so the sample can be diverted before the batch is filled.

What happens if a parcel is delayed or seized in customs?

We reship or credit. Express consignments are tracked end to end; if a parcel is held for documentation we supply replacement paperwork the same day, and if it is lost or destroyed in transit we replace the goods on the next available batch.

Need the COA for a specific batch?

Tell us the compound and strength — we will send the current batch certificate and chromatogram the same working day.
