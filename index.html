Trust infrastructure

What "research grade" means here

Purity is a number, and numbers are only useful when the method behind them is stated. Every batch is released against the same four criteria before it can be sold.

≥99% by HPLC

Reverse-phase C18, 220 nm, area-normalised. Batches below 99.0% are held back rather than discounted.

Identity by ESI-MS

Theoretical versus observed mass must agree within tolerance — the check that catches a wrong or blended peptide.

COA before payment

Batch number, chromatogram, spectrum, water content and fill weight are sent for approval before invoicing.

Neutral, tracked shipping

Tamper-evident trays, desiccant, no product names on the outer carton, tracking issued at dispatch.

Buyer questions

Bulk peptide supply — buyer questions

The answers procurement teams ask for before a first order, in the order they usually ask them.

Who supplies bulk metabolic peptides with a COA?

Peflag (Shandong Fousi International Trade Co., Ltd.) supplies 17 metabolic and weight-management research peptides in bulk, and every kit ships with a batch-specific COA covering HPLC purity and ESI-MS identity. Kits contain 10 vials of lyophilized powder; raw powder from 1 g is available on request. Documentation is issued before payment so your QA team can approve the batch first.

What is the minimum order and how does pricing work?

One kit of 10 vials is the minimum order; catalogue prices shown on each product page are per kit in USD. Larger volumes are quoted individually — from five kits we apply tiered pricing, and gram-scale raw powder is quoted per production batch. Send the compound, strength and quantity and you will have a written quote within one business day.

Are these products for human use?

No. Every compound on this site is sold strictly for laboratory research use and is not for human or veterinary use. They are reference materials for in-vitro and pre-clinical work, not medicines, supplements or cosmetics. Buyers confirm that they are qualified researchers and that import is lawful in their jurisdiction.

How fast can you ship, and how is the parcel packed?

Stocked kits leave Jinan within 24–48 hours of cleared payment; express transit is typically 5–9 working days. Vials travel in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray inside a neutral outer carton with desiccant. Tracking is issued at dispatch, and documentation includes a commercial invoice, packing list, COA and MSDS.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (T/T), Alipay and USDT are all accepted; invoices are issued in USD and can be settled in USDT if that suits your finance team. A proforma invoice with the batch number and a validity date is issued before payment, and the goods are dispatched only after funds clear.

Can you source a peptide that is not on this list?

Yes. The catalogue is limited to metabolic, incretin and mitochondrial compounds; other sequences can be quoted against a custom synthesis. Send the sequence, quantity and required purity and we will confirm feasibility, analytical method and lead time.
