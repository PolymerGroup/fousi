Buyer questions

Comparison FAQ

Which receptor does each peptide class target?

GLP-1 agonists target GLP-1R; dual agonists add GIPR; triple agonists add GCGR; amylin analogues target CTR/RAMP complexes. Semaglutide and liraglutide are GLP-1 selective, tirzepatide is GIP/GLP-1, retatrutide is GIP/GLP-1/glucagon, and cagrilintide is amylin selective.

What is the difference between tirzepatide and retatrutide?

Tirzepatide engages GIP and GLP-1 receptors; retatrutide adds glucagon-receptor agonism on the same C20-diacid architecture. Tirzepatide free base is 4813.50 Da and retatrutide is 4731.33 Da, so the two are distinguishable by mass spectrometry as well as by pharmacology.
