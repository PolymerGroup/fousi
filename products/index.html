Choosing Compound

How these four mechanisms differ in the lab

See the full comparison table

Incretin agonism — GLP-1, GIP, glucagon

Single, dual and triple agonists differ in how many receptors they engage. Semaglutide and liraglutide are GLP-1 selective; tirzepatide adds GIP; retatrutide, survodutide and mazdutide add glucagon. The more arms, the more you need a clean comparator: most programmes run a GLP-1-only control alongside the multi-agonist.

Amylin signalling — cagrilintide and CagriSema

Amylin acts on hindbrain CTR/RAMP complexes rather than incretin receptors. Cagrilintide isolates that pathway; CagriSema combines it with semaglutide in a fixed ratio for additivity studies.

Mitochondrial & energy sensing — MOTS-c, SS-31, AICAR, humanin, 5-Amino-1MQ

These act downstream of appetite: AMPK activation, cardiolipin stabilisation, NAD⁺ availability and mitochondrial-derived peptide signalling. They are the natural pairing for respirometry and energy-expenditure read-outs.

Lipolytic — AOD9604, adipotide, tesamorelin

Three different routes to fat metabolism: an hGH fragment that avoids GH-receptor signalling, a prohibitin-targeted peptide that acts on adipose vasculature, and a GHRH analogue that works upstream of endogenous GH release.
