What Tesamorelin is

Tesamorelin is a 44-residue growth-hormone-releasing hormone analogue. The single modification — a trans-3-hexenoyl group on the N-terminus — blocks DPP-4 from clipping the first two residues, extending the half-life to around 26 minutes and producing a more physiological pulsatile GH release than exogenous GH itself.

Its research niche is visceral adipose tissue. Unlike systemic GH, which affects fat and lean mass broadly, GHRH-driven GH release has been studied for a comparatively selective effect on visceral depots, together with effects on hepatic fat and inflammatory markers. It is also used in pituitary-function and GH-axis studies where a defined GHRH stimulus is required.

Mechanism of action

Tesamorelin binds the GHRH receptor on anterior pituitary somatotrophs, stimulating cAMP and GH secretion. Because it acts upstream of the GH receptor, the downstream increase in IGF-1 is endogenous and pulsatile. Visceral adipocytes are particularly responsive to GH-mediated lipolysis, which is the rationale for studying the compound in visceral-fat endpoints rather than general body-weight change.

Sourcing note. The N-terminal hexenoyl group is what separates this from native GHRH(1-44), and the deconvoluted mass on the COA shows the modification is actually present.

Purity verification, MOQ, packaging, shipping and payment — answered directly.

How does tesamorelin differ from a growth-hormone secretagogue such as ipamorelin?
They act at different receptors. Tesamorelin is a GHRH-receptor agonist acting directly on somatotrophs; ipamorelin is a ghrelin-receptor agonist acting on a separate hypothalamic/pituitary pathway. Studies sometimes combine them to test synergistic GH release.
Is the trans-3-hexenoyl group visible in your MS data?
Yes — it changes the monoisotopic mass by 98 Da relative to unmodified GHRH(1-44), and the deconvoluted mass on the COA confirms the modified sequence rather than the parent peptide.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?

Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name.
