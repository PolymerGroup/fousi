What Semaglutide is

Semaglutide is a 31-residue GLP-1 analogue built on the human GLP-1 (7-37) backbone. Two sequence edits do most of the pharmacological work: an α-aminoisobutyric acid (Aib) residue at position 8 blocks DPP-4 cleavage, and Lys26 carries a C18 fatty diacid attached through an AEEA-AEEA-γGlu linker. That lipid chain drives strong, reversible albumin binding, which slows renal clearance and extends the plasma half-life to roughly 165 hours in humans — long enough for once-weekly dosing.

For laboratory work, semaglutide is the standard against which newer dual and triple agonists are compared. Typical read-outs include GLP-1R-mediated cAMP accumulation, β-arrestin recruitment, glucose-dependent insulin secretion in islet preparations, gastric emptying and hypothalamic appetite signalling. Because the molecule is well characterised, it is also the usual positive control in receptor-binding and analytical-method development work.

Mechanism of action

Semaglutide binds the extracellular domain of GLP-1R and stabilises an active conformation of the class B GPCR, promoting Gs-mediated cAMP production and PKA-dependent signalling. Albumin binding creates a slowly equilibrating depot in plasma, so free peptide exposure stays stable for days. Downstream effects studied in research models include potentiated glucose-stimulated insulin release, suppressed glucagon during hyperglycaemia, delayed gastric emptying and reduced food intake via brainstem and hypothalamic GLP-1R populations.

Research applications

  • DDP-4 resistant backbone. Aib8 substitution and C-terminal amide protection give a peptide that survives plasma protease exposure — a practical advantage for long-incubation assays.
  • Albumin-bound exposure. The C18 diacid linker supports once-weekly pharmacokinetics and a flat exposure profile, which simplifies chronic study design.
  • Benchmark comparator. The reference molecule for dual GIP/GLP-1 and triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon programmes, useful for potency normalisation across batches.
  • Two-compartment stability. Lyophilised powder is stable at -20 °C for long-term storage and tolerates short ambient transit when kept dry.

Analytical release data

Each Semaglutide kit is released against four measurable criteria, and the numbers travel with the goods:

  • Purity — ≥99.0% by reversed-phase HPLC at 220 nm, area normalisation against the main peak.
  • Identity — ESI-MS deconvoluted mass must match the theoretical mass of 4113.58 g/mol within 0.1%.
  • Water content — ≤5.0% by Karl Fischer, so the weighed powder is peptide, not moisture.
  • Fill weight verified per vial across the kit, with the observed range reported on the COA.

Is your semaglutide free base or acetate salt?
We stock the free base by default, and the counter-ion actually present in the batch is stated on the COA. If your assay requires the acetate form, say so in the inquiry and we will confirm availability and lead time before invoicing.
Can I get a semaglutide reference standard with a known potency?
Yes. For method development we can supply a small qualification sample alongside the commercial kit, with the same batch number, HPLC chromatogram and MS spectrum so that identity and purity can be documented in your own lab records.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?

Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name; that adds roughly five working days and a testing fee.

What is the minimum order quantity and how does bulk pricing work?

The minimum is one kit of 10 vials. Catalogue prices are per kit and already include the batch COA. From five kits we apply tiered pricing, and from twenty kits we quote against a dedicated production run with a reserved batch number so your reorders match. Raw lyophilized powder is available from 1 g in 1 g, 5 g and 10 g aliquots, priced per batch rather than per kit.

How is Semaglutide packed and how long does delivery take?
