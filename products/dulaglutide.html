What Dulaglutide is

Dulaglutide solves the short-half-life problem differently from semaglutide: instead of a lipid chain, it attaches a GLP-1 analogue to a modified human IgG4 Fc domain. The Fc region engages the neonatal Fc receptor, recycling the fusion protein and slowing clearance. The result is a ~63 kDa glycoprotein with a half-life approaching five days and a much larger hydrodynamic radius than an acylated peptide.

That size difference matters experimentally. Dulaglutide is useful when a study needs to compare small-peptide and large-molecule GLP-1 agonists, to examine Fc-receptor-mediated recycling, or to work with an agonist whose exposure profile is driven by protein engineering rather than albumin binding.

Mechanism of action

The GLP-1 portion binds and activates GLP-1R, raising intracellular cAMP and potentiating glucose-dependent insulin secretion. The Fc domain binds FcRn, which rescues the molecule from lysosomal degradation and returns it to circulation, extending exposure. Because the molecule is glycosylated and large, tissue distribution differs from that of acylated peptides — a factor worth controlling for in comparative pharmacology.

Sourcing note. This is a glycoprotein, so the release package carries the expression-system statement and SEC-MALS or SDS-PAGE data instead of a peptide mass trace.

Research applications

  • FcRn-mediated recycling. A textbook example of half-life extension by Fc fusion rather than lipidation.
  • Large-molecule comparator. Contrasts cleanly with small acylated peptides in distribution and clearance studies.
  • Glycosylated product. Relevant to analytical work on glycoprotein characterisation and batch consistency.
  • Weekly exposure. Supports once-weekly study designs without frequent handling.

Analytical release data

Each Dulaglutide kit is released against four measurable criteria, and the numbers travel with the goods:

  • Purity — ≥99.0% by reversed-phase HPLC at 220 nm, area normalisation against the main peak.
  • Identity — ESI-MS deconvoluted mass must match the theoretical mass of ≈63,000 Da within 0.1%.
  • Water content — ≤5.0% by Karl Fischer, so the weighed powder is peptide, not moisture.
  • Fill weight verified per vial across the kit, with the observed range reported on the COA.

Third-party laboratory verification can be arranged on request for an additional fee, with the report issued in your institution's name. Read the full QC protocol.

Packaging, storage and handling

Kits ship as ten 10 mL serum vials in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray. Lyophilized powder is stable at ambient temperature for short transit; on arrival, store at -20 °C, protected from light, and allow the vial to reach room temperature before opening to avoid condensation. Reconstitute with the buffer specified in your protocol, not with water alone if your assay requires salts, and prepare single-use aliquots to avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

Buyer questions

Dulaglutide — bulk buyer FAQ

Purity verification, MOQ, packaging, shipping and payment — answered directly.

Is your dulaglutide expressed in mammalian cells?
Yes. An Fc-fusion glycoprotein of this size cannot be produced by solid-phase synthesis; it is expressed in mammalian cells and purified. The COA states the expression system and the measured molecular weight by SEC-MALS or SDS-PAGE.
How should dulaglutide be handled compared with a lyophilized peptide?
Treat it as a protein: avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles, keep working solutions cold, and use low-binding tubes. The lyophilized powder ships at ambient temperature but should be stored at -20 °C on arrival.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?

Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name; that adds roughly five working days and a testing fee.

What is the minimum order quantity and how does bulk pricing work?

The minimum is one kit of 10 vials. Catalogue prices are per kit and already include the batch COA. From five kits we apply tiered pricing, and from twenty kits we quote against a dedicated production run with a reserved batch number so your reorders match. Raw lyophilized powder is available from 1 g in 1 g, 5 g and 10 g aliquots, priced per batch rather than per kit.

How is Dulaglutide packed and how long does delivery take?

Stocked kits leave Jinan within 24–48 hours; express transit is typically 5–9 working days. Ten vials travel in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray with desiccant inside a neutral outer carton that names no product. We ship by DHL, FedEx or UPS with tracking issued at dispatch, and the parcel includes a commercial invoice, packing list, COA and MSDS. If a shipment is held or lost in transit we reship or credit — that is stated on the invoice.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (T/T), Alipay and USDT are accepted; the proforma is issued in USD and can be settled in USDT. A proforma invoice stating the compound, strength, kit count, batch number and validity date is issued first.
