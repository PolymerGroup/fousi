What Humanin is

Humanin was the first mitochondrial-derived peptide discovered. The 24-residue sequence is encoded within the mitochondrial 16S rRNA region and acts as a cytoprotective signal in both intracellular and extracellular compartments. It was originally identified by its ability to protect neurons from amyloid-induced apoptosis, and has since been studied across metabolic, cardiovascular and neurodegeneration models.

Metabolically, humanin has been reported to improve insulin sensitivity and to act on hypothalamic circuits regulating appetite. Mechanistically it interacts with IGFBP-3 and blocks Bax-mediated apoptosis, and it has been shown to enter cells and bind intracellular targets. Humanin analogues such as HNG are often used alongside the native sequence in structure-activity work.

Mechanism of action

Humanin binds the IGFBP-3/IGF-1R axis and interferes with Bax activation, reducing cytochrome c release and caspase-dependent apoptosis. In peripheral tissue models it enhances insulin-stimulated glucose uptake and Akt phosphorylation. The peptide also acts centrally on hypothalamic nuclei to reduce food intake, linking mitochondrial stress signalling to whole-body energy balance.

Sourcing note. Cysteine-containing and prone to dimerisation: store lyophilised, prepare fresh, and read the reduced/oxidised ratio on the COA before starting.

Do you supply humanin analogues such as HNG?
The catalogue carries the native 24-residue humanin sequence. Analogues can be sourced on request against a production batch; tell us the exact sequence and quantity and we will confirm feasibility and lead time.
Is humanin stable enough for cell-culture work?
The peptide is cysteine-containing and can dimerise on prolonged storage, so keep it lyophilised at -20 °C and prepare fresh working solution. The COA reports both the reduced and oxidised forms where they separate chromatographically.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?

Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name.
