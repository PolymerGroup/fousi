What AOD9604 is

AOD9604 corresponds to the tail end of the human growth hormone sequence, residues 176 to 191, with a disulfide bridge between the two cysteine residues. This fragment retains the lipolytic activity attributed to the parent hormone but does not bind the growth hormone receptor, so studies using it can separate fat-metabolism effects from the IGF-1 and growth effects of full-length hGH.

The same molecule appears in supplier catalogues under both names — AOD9604 and HGH fragment 176-191 — with different strength ladders and different prices. Peflag consolidates both into a single SKU family so buyers can order any strength from 1 mg to 15 mg without guessing which listing is current.

Mechanism of action

AOD9604 is studied as a lipolytic agent: in adipocyte models it increases glycerol and free fatty-acid release and appears to act downstream of β-adrenergic signalling rather than through the GH receptor. Because it lacks the GH receptor-binding domains, it does not raise IGF-1 in the way full-length hGH does — the distinction that motivates most comparative work with this fragment.

Sourcing note. Both catalogue names — AOD9604 and hGH fragment 176-191 — are filled from the identical sequence, so the strength you select never changes the molecule.

Analytical release data

Each AOD9604 kit is released against four measurable criteria, and the numbers travel with the goods:

  • Purity — ≥99.0% by reversed-phase HPLC at 220 nm, area normalisation against the main peak.
  • Identity — ESI-MS deconvoluted mass must match the theoretical mass of 1815.10 g/mol within 0.1%.
  • Water content — ≤5.0% by Karl Fischer, so the weighed powder is peptide, not moisture.
  • Fill weight verified per vial across the kit, with the observed range reported on the COA.

AOD9604 — bulk buyer FAQ

Is AOD9604 the same as HGH fragment 176-191?
Yes. Both names refer to the 16-residue C-terminal hGH fragment with CAS 221231-10-3. We list them on one page because the two catalogue names had separate price lists for the same molecule, which caused duplicate orders and inconsistent documentation.
How do you confirm the disulfide bond is intact?
Mass spectrometry alone cannot distinguish a bridged from a reduced peptide at the same nominal mass, so we compare reduced and non-reduced LC-MS runs. The COA reports both traces when a batch is released for receptor or cell work.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?
Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name; that adds roughly five working days and a testing fee.
What is the minimum order quantity and how does bulk pricing work?
The minimum is one kit of 10 vials. Catalogue prices are per kit and already include the batch COA. From five kits we apply tiered pricing, and from twenty kits we quote against a dedicated production run with a reserved batch number so your reorders match. Raw lyophilized powder is available from 1 g in 1 g, 5 g and 10 g aliquots, priced per batch rather than per kit.
How is AOD9604 packed and how long does delivery take?
Stocked kits leave Jinan within 24–48 hours; express transit is typically 5–9 working days. Ten vials travel in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray with desiccant inside a neutral outer carton that names no product. We ship by DHL, FedEx or UPS with tracking issued at dispatch, and the parcel includes a commercial invoice, packing list, COA and MSDS. If a shipment is held or lost in transit we reship or credit — that is stated on the invoice.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer in USD (T/T) is standard; Alipay and USDT are accepted for smaller orders. A proforma invoice stating the compound, strength, kit count, batch number and validity date is issued first. Goods are dispatched only after funds clear, and the commercial invoice accompanies the shipment for customs.
