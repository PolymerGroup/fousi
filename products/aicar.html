What AICAR is

AICAR (5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleotide) is a cell-permeable adenosine analogue. Inside the cell it is phosphorylated to ZMP, which mimics AMP at the AMPK γ-subunit and activates the kinase without changing cellular ATP. That mechanism makes it the standard pharmacological control for AMPK activation studies.

AICAR is included in the Peflag metabolic catalogue because it is routinely requested alongside MOTS-c, SS-31 and 5-Amino-1MQ: it provides the pharmacological AMPK arm that those compounds are often compared against. It is a small molecule, not a peptide, and is supplied as a powder for reconstitution.

Mechanism of action

AICAR enters cells through adenosine transporters, is phosphorylated by adenosine kinase to ZMP, and ZMP binds the AMPK γ-subunit at the AMP-binding site. This allosterically activates AMPK and promotes phosphorylation of downstream targets such as ACC, ULK1 and PGC-1α. The resulting shift toward fatty-acid oxidation and glucose uptake is the basis of its exercise-mimetic phenotype in rodent models.

Sourcing note. A small molecule, not a peptide — listed here because it is the standard AMPK control ordered alongside MOTS-c and SS-31.

Research applications

  • Reference AMPK control. The standard comparator for any compound claimed to act through AMPK.
  • ZMP mechanism. Activates AMPK without altering the cellular ATP/ADP ratio — a cleaner interpretation than energetic stress.
  • Pairs with mitochondrial peptides. Used alongside MOTS-c and SS-31 to separate AMPK-dependent from membrane-level effects.
  • Two pack sizes. 50 mg and 100 mg for dose-response and chronic work.

Analytical release data

Each AICAR kit is released against four measurable criteria, and the numbers travel with the goods:

  • Purity — ≥99.0% by reversed-phase HPLC at 220 nm, area normalisation against the main peak.
  • Identity — ESI-MS deconvoluted mass must match the theoretical mass of 258.23 g/mol within 0.1%.
  • Water content — ≤5.0% by Karl Fischer, so the weighed powder is peptide, not moisture.
  • Fill weight verified per vial across the kit, with the observed range reported on the COA.

Third-party laboratory verification can be arranged on request for an additional fee, with the report issued in your institution's name. Read the full QC protocol.

Packaging, storage and handling

Kits ship as ten 10 mL serum vials in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray. Lyophilized powder is stable at ambient temperature for short transit; on arrival, store at -20 °C, protected from light, and allow the vial to reach room temperature before opening to avoid condensation. Reconstitute with the buffer specified in your protocol, not with water alone if your assay requires salts, and prepare single-use aliquots to avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

Buyer questions
AICAR — bulk buyer FAQ
Purity verification, MOQ, packaging, shipping and payment — answered directly.

    
Why is AICAR in a peptide catalogue?
Because researchers order it with the mitochondrial peptides. It is not a peptide and we say so on the page — it is a small-molecule AMPK activator supplied as research-grade powder.
How should AICAR be dissolved?
It is water-soluble but dissolves slowly; prepare a stock in warm buffer or dilute base and adjust pH back to neutral before use. Avoid prolonged heating, which hydrolyses the ribonucleotide.
How do I verify purity and identity before ordering?
Ask for the current batch COA — it is sent before payment, not after. The document carries the batch number, an HPLC chromatogram with area-normalised purity, an ESI-MS spectrum with the deconvoluted mass, Karl Fischer water content and the fill-weight range. If your QA group requires independent confirmation, we can arrange third-party testing at an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory and issue the report in your institution's name; that adds roughly five working days and a testing fee.
What is the minimum order quantity and how does bulk pricing work?
The minimum is one kit of 10 vials. Catalogue prices are per kit and already include the batch COA. From five kits we apply tiered pricing, and from twenty kits we quote against a dedicated production run with a reserved batch number so your reorders match. Raw lyophilized powder is available from 1 g in 1 g, 5 g and 10 g aliquots, priced per batch rather than per kit.
How is AICAR packed and how long does delivery take?
Stocked kits leave Jinan within 24–48 hours; express transit is typically 5–9 working days. Ten vials travel in a tamper-evident, shock-absorbing tray with desiccant inside a neutral outer carton that names no product. We ship by DHL, FedEx or UPS with tracking issued at dispatch, and the parcel includes a commercial invoice, packing list, COA and MSDS. If a shipment is held or lost in transit we reship or credit — that is stated on the invoice.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer (T/T), Alipay and USDT are accepted; the proforma is issued in USD and can be settled in USDT. A proforma invoice stating the compound, strength, kit count, batch number and validity date is issued first. Goods are dispatched only after funds clear, and the commercial invoice accompanies the shipment for customs.
